__________________________________________________-Available KAB-________________________________________________________
_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________



..........Any map editing, will tempoarily create graphical errors, 
..........on the Title Screen, Menu Screen, and Bahamut Graphics.
..........(Repair Title and Bahamut) will completely fix this each time.

..........Make sure you have above (3043 KAB). If it goes lower, then some of the Standard Maps
..........will be effected when you use (Repair Title and Bahamut).

..........Out of around 48000 KAB total, 3043 less isnt too bad for the addition of Bahamut.

*************************************************************************************************************************

___________________________________________________-New Features-________________________________________________________
_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________



..........There is a nice extra feature in GFF, that gives more options when choosing Palettes.
..........With the 3rd set of Palettes (going from top to bottom), they are assigned to a new feature.
..........This new feature is having 5 Palette sequences to cycle through.
..........Along with the new cycling effect, it is also usually connected with another added feature, animated tiles.
..........Some of the tiles now have animated effects, which adds tremendously to the aesthetics of the game.

..........Getting the right Palette combo to make it look good is a little tricky, but definately adds new life to FF1.


..........Here are a few examples of where this new feature is used.

..........Animated ocean, along with cycling, gives a moving shoreline effect.
..........Animated lava tiles and cycling.
..........Animated ice tiles and cycling.

..........Then there are the animated tiles with no cycling.
..........Rivers in castles and towns, and water in any Sea Shrine area.

*************************************************************************************************************************

___________________________________________________-Palette Sets-________________________________________________________
_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________



..........In Hackster, when you double click on (any tile), you are given the choice to assign that tile 
..........to any one of the 4 Palette sets. Each set consists of 3 colors, and all sets share 1 common color.
..........This common color is usually black for Standard Maps, but with the Overworld Map it is green.

..........Even though the Standard Map Palettes were moved, you can still assign the 4 Palette sets,
..........to each tile with Hackster. If you choose the 3rd set, just make sure it will work out
..........the way you want on that given map.

_________________________________________________-Palette Options-_______________________________________________________
_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________



..........For starters, Standard Map Palettes were moved from 2010/2b7f to 27010/27f4f.
..........GFF adds a 4th row, which are the 5 Palette sets that will cycle if you choose.

..........The in game Mini Map was switched around with Standard Map Palettes in GFF. 
..........The 2 patches (Temporary Map Palettes) and (Repair Mini Map), are because of this fact.

..........If you decide to change a bunch of Palettes, then its probably best to never use (Repair Mini Map),
..........until you are done with your hack. Using (Temporary Map Palettes) would patch over any new choices,
..........that would be visible through Hackster. If you dont change any Palettes, 
..........then switching back and forth between these 2 patches will never be an issue. 
                                
..........While (Temporary Map Palettes) is in effect, the Mini Map will not work at all.
..........Use (View Mini Map) in Hackster, which is not effected, except for the colors.


..........Here is the layout, and what to look for.

..........Coneria

27010-----0F 1A 28 18 - 0F 00 1A 10 - 0F 30 2C 1C - 0F 00 02 30-----Standard
27020-----0F 0F 12 36 - 0F 0F 27 36 - 0F 0F 27 36 - 0F 0F 16 36-----Control/Sprites
27030-----0F 1A 28 18 - 0F 00 1A 10 - 0F 30 2C 1C - 0F 00 02 30-----In Rooms
27040-----0F (30 2C 1C)(30 2C 1C)(30 2C 1C)(30 2C 1C)(30 2C 1C)-----Palettes that will cycle

..........As you see, the 3rd Palette set 27019/1b and 27039/3b are 30 2C 1C.
..........On the last row, 27040, it is repeated all 5 times, which means nothing cycles on this map.


..........Waterfall

27490-----0F 31 31 31 - 0F 31 31 21 - 0F 11 21 31 - 0F 00 02 3D-----Standard
274a0-----0F 0F 12 36 - 0F 0F 27 36 - 0F 08 18 15 - 0F 08 17 15-----Control/Sprites
274b0-----0F 10 17 0F - 0F 0F 11 11 - 0F 11 21 31 - 0F 00 02 3D-----In Rooms
274c0-----0F (31 11 21)(21 31 11)(11 21 31)(31 11 21)(21 31 11)-----Palettes that will cycle

..........On this map, you can see that 11 21 31 cycles through, giving a moving water effect.


..........Overworld Map Palettes can be chosen completely with Hackster, but if you change the 3rd set, or assign
..........a tile to the 3rd set, then you will need to make sure you have the desired results.
..........The 5 cycling Palettes, just like any other map, has to be done with a Hex Editor.
