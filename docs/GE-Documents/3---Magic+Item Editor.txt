_____________________________________________________-Overview-__________________________________________________________
_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________



..........Everything concerning the Magic and Attack Editor has to be done with a Hex editor. Initially it seemed
..........that some of it could be done with Hackster, but data gets shifted around and therefore cant be used.

..........Editing Magic is a little more complicating then the other sections. If you are just going to change the Power 
..........of any direct type Spells like Cure, Fire, or Protect, then this will be easy. Creating your own Spells will
..........require you to pay close attention to how each Spell functions. 

..........Direct Damage/Healing Magic - Effectivity is the strength of the Spell, and Hit% is set high to always work.

..........Status Inflicting/Resistance Magic - Effectivity is a Checkbox Option, which is listed in that section below.

..........Spells that use a Checkbox Option instead of having Direct Damage or Healing for Effectivity will have a (star)*
..........A few exceptions will be both Direct and a Checkbox Option, which will have (two stars)**

..........On the right of certain Spells you will see the Outside of Battle command associated with it. If you move where
..........these are assigned, then you have to change the data in that spell slot to match it.

3a4f0-Item Cap

There are 2 catches with the Item Cap.

FF gives a cap of 254, and 00 doesnt give you a 255 cap. 00 and 01 will not allow you to hold any items.
There is a rollover effect when you use the "Buy 10" option.
 
Buy 100 and get A0, then again for K0, and again gives you 44.
K0=200. Add 56, then 44 more, and its add 100 to get 44.

However, when you buy individual items one at a time, it will cap off at the desired amount.

..........Abbreviations - Green or Grey, and Blue or Black. These will be fully spelled out at least once per byte.
..........If the colors are (Blue,BlueGreen,LtBlGr), then Bl and Gr will clearly be Blue and Green. Occasionally I will
..........only use the first letter because of space restraints. DkPurpleRed,LtLtPR,LtPR. PR is PurpleRed, Pr is Purple.

..........Each set of bytes 00-0f, 10-1f, 20-2f, and 30-3f, each display colors in a different sequence.
..........There are similarities in each set. 05, 15, 25, and 35 all have PurpleRed in them, and 08, 18, 28, and 38 
..........have GreenOrange/GreenBrown. As you are looking for the color you want, follow this pattern.
