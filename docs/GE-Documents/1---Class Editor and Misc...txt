----------HIT POINTS----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


2d9b6/b7-HP Max

e8 03 = 999 | 38 18 = 6199 | 10 27 = 9999 | ff ff = 65535

Displays in battle only, with up to 9P5=1155 showing out of battle
 
(A00=1000, a00=3600, z00=6100). After that, you cant tell what the values are



2dcd8-Strong HP Gain Low

2dcda-Strong HP Gain High


----------MAGIC POINTS--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


2dca6-Max MP Cap for Mages.....byte value is 1 more than real amount. 0a=9

2dcaa-Max MP Cap for Kn/Ni.....byte value is 1 more than real amount. 05=4


2dc91-MP up XX instead of 1


MP on level up for FI
2dc7d/82 > ea ea c9 01 f0 3c

MP on level up for TH
2dc7d/82 > f0 40 c9 01 ea ea

MP on level up for both Fi and TH
2dc7d/82 > ea ea c9 01 ea ea 

Note: There are no restrictions for BB/MA. They can earn MP on any level up

2dd35-Max stats

64=100 - fe=255 - ff=0
